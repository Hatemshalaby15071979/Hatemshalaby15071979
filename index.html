Operating principles

  • Evidence before claims. A decision is gated, recorded, and inspectable before it runs.
  • Offline by default. Tools run on the customer's own machine; no network calls in the evidence path, no credentials, no production touch.
  • Human in the loop. Nothing is saved, sent, or executed before explicit approval. The machine never auto-signs or classifies a system as high-risk.
  • Honest about limits. Synthetic fixtures are labelled; production gates stay red until external evidence is supplied.
